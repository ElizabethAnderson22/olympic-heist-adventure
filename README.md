# Olympic Heist Adventure

Olympic Heist Adventure is a Java-based text adventure game in which players explore an Olympic stadium, solve puzzles, collect useful items and attempt to steal a prized Olympic gold medal.

The project was originally developed during my first year studying Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at the University of Birmingham and has since been refined and documented as part of my software portfolio.

---

## Overview

The objective of the game is to successfully navigate through a series of interconnected rooms within an Olympic stadium. Along the way, players must discover hidden items, solve puzzles, interact with their surroundings and overcome obstacles before completing the final heist.

The game is entirely text-based, encouraging players to think strategically and explore carefully in order to uncover the correct sequence of actions required to win.

---

## Features

- Explore interconnected rooms
- Collect and manage inventory items
- Solve puzzles to unlock progression
- Discover hidden routes and useful tools
- Complete the Olympic medal heist
- Track your score throughout the game
